Hosting a game? Start here

Player manual

THE 14 is a quiz party game for up to 14 friends, each on their own phone. The host shares a link, you tap it, and every question lands on every phone at the same moment. Answer fast, answer right, and keep the banter going on voice chat.

  • 1,800+ questions
  • 44 categories
  • 13 ways to play
  • 14 players max

The short version

  1. Tap the invite link the host sends to your group chat.
  2. Pick a name and build a character. Then wait in the lobby.
  3. Join voice so you can hear everyone while you play.
  4. Answer on your phone before the timer runs out. Right and fast scores the most.
  5. Most points after the last question wins.

Get in

Join a game

All you need is your phone and a decent connection. No app to download, no account to make.

  1. Tap the invite link

    The host drops a link in your group chat. It opens the join page with the game code already filled in.

    Host

    Join my THE 14 game. Code DHUCS

    the-14.vercel.app/join?code=DHUCS 20:41

    Only got the code? Open the-14.vercel.app/join and type the five letters.

  2. Pick a name

    First names work best. If someone in the game already has your name, you are asked to add an initial.

  3. Build your character

    Seven styles, eight background colours and endless looks. Tap Show more looks until one feels like you. Your character reacts all game: it smiles when you are right and sulks when you are wrong.

  4. Wait in the lobby

    You see everyone who has joined. When the host starts, the first question counts down on every phone at once.

Talk while you play

Voice chat

Half the fun is the noise. The game has free voice chat built in, so you can hear every groan and celebration from wherever everyone is.

  1. VoiceBottom right of your screen.
  2. Join voiceIn the panel that opens.
  3. AllowWhen your phone asks for the microphone.

Up to 8 people

Phones connect straight to each other. Use the Mute button whenever you need to.

Quiet during questions

Mics mute while a question is live, so nobody can shout the answer. They open again for the reveal.

Hard to hear?

Turn on Speaker to make everyone louder. Earphones give the best sound and stop echo.

More than 8 of you?

The host can add a WhatsApp, Meet or Discord call link. A Join call button then shows for everyone.

Shows "can't connect"?

Some networks block direct calls. Use the host's call link instead, or switch between Wi-Fi and mobile data.

On an iPhone

The mic usually pauses when your screen locks. Keep the screen on while you play.

How it flows

What happens in a round

Everything plays out on your phone. You never need to see the host's screen.

One question in the ready made games, drawn to scale. The host can change the answer time.

  1. Get ready

    A 3, 2, 1 countdown on every phone at once. It shows the question number, the mode and the category.

    3.5s

  2. Answer

    The question and options appear with a timer bar. Answers unlock half a second in, so a stray tap never counts. Your first answer is final.

    20s

  3. Reveal

    The right answer, how everyone answered and what you scored. Send a reaction while it is up.

    6 to 9s

  4. Standings

    The leaderboard, with arrows for who moved up or down.

    Every 3 questions

  5. Podium

    The top three, awards like Sharpest mind and Master bluffer, and a tag for every player. Save your result card for your status or story.

    After the last one

How you win

Points, speed and streaks

Every question has a level, and harder ones are worth more. The level is never shown, so you never know when a big one is coming.

Easy100Warm ups and crowd pleasers
Medium150You probably know it
Hard200+For the know-it-alls

Points for a right answer. A wrong answer or no answer scores 0, and your score never goes below zero.

Speed bonus +50%

The fastest right answer gets half the question's points on top. Second fastest gets a quarter, third gets an eighth.

Streak bonus +50

Get three right in a row and every right answer from then on earns 50 extra. One miss ends the streak.

Too fast < 1.2s

Tap in under 1.2 seconds and the game calls it out. It does not change your points, only your reputation.

Fair timing

The game times every answer from the moment the question opens, so nobody can fake a faster tap.

The last question

Final Wager

When the host switches it on, the last question becomes a bet. It can win you the game, or lose it right before the podium.

  • Bet before you see it. You only see the category, then you choose your stake.
  • Up to half your points. Everyone can bet at least 200, so even last place has a shot.
  • Change your mind. Move your bet as often as you like until betting closes.
  • Then answer. Right: the question's points plus your bet. Wrong or no answer: you lose your bet.

Switches the host can turn on

Team Battle and Power-ups

Two extras that change how a game feels. Use one, both or neither.

Team Battle

In the lobby, tap Team Amber or Team Teal. You can switch sides until the game starts, and anyone who has not picked joins the smaller team.

How teams win

Teams are ranked by average points per player, so a smaller team is never at a disadvantage. A team bar shows on the standings and the podium names the winning team.

Power-ups

Get three right in a row and you earn a random card. You hold one at a time. Play it from your phone between questions and it works on the next question. Every card played pops up on everyone's screen.

❄️

Freeze

Pick a player. Their answers stay locked for the first 5 seconds.

🫳

Steal

Take 50 points from the player just above you. In the lead? You take from second place.

🔀

🛡️

Nothing to tap. It blocks the next card anyone plays on you.

✌️

A bet on yourself. Right answer counts double, wrong costs 100.

Playing from different places

Online tips

A few habits that keep the game smooth when everyone is somewhere else.

Use a steady connection

Your time counts when your tap reaches the game. A laggy network can cost you the speed bonus, so Wi-Fi or strong data helps.

Stay on the game

Leave the page for a while and you show as away. Away players do not hold up the reveal and are left out of Most Likely To.

Dropped out?

Tap the invite link again on the same phone and you get your seat and score back. On another phone, join with the same name.

Opened inside WhatsApp?

The game works there, but Chrome or Safari gives you full screen. From there you can add it to your home screen.

React out loud

Tap the emoji bar on the reveal and standings screens. Everyone sees your reaction float up with your name.

Keep your card

At the end, save or share a story-size result card with your place, your tag and your stats.

Questions people ask

Quick answers

Do I need to see the host's screen?

No. The question, the options, the timer, the reveal and the standings all show on your phone.

Do I need to install anything?

No. It runs in your phone's browser. For full screen play you can add it to your home screen: on iPhone, open it in Safari and tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. On Android, tap Install when Chrome offers it.

Can I change my answer?

No. Your first answer is final, so be sure before you tap. The exceptions are your Final Wager bet, which you can change until betting closes, and Sketch, where you can keep guessing until you get it.

Why did someone get more points for the same answer?

Speed and streaks. The three fastest right answers earn a bonus, and a streak of three or more adds 50 per question. Two people with the same answer can end up with different scores.

Can I tell which Bluff answer is real from how it is typed?

No. Every answer is shown in the same style: a capital first letter, no full stop at the end and no shouting in capitals. You have to read your friends, not the keyboard.

What happens if I do not answer?

You get no points for that question and your streak ends. In the Final Wager, no answer loses your bet, so answer something.

Can we play Sketch if we are not in the same room?

Yes. The drawing shows up live on every player's phone, so you do not need a shared screen. The artist's phone sends the drawing, so they should stay on a steady connection. If you reload, the drawing so far comes back.

Someone froze me. Did I lose the question?

No. You just have to wait out the first 5 seconds, then answer as normal. The speed bonus goes to the fastest players, though, so a freeze can cost you that.

Does the host see the answers early?

No. The host sees each question at the same time as everyone else. The answer only appears at the reveal.

  • Sports: football, basketball, motorsport, wrestling and more
  • Kenya & Africa

How do I host a game?

Open the-14.vercel.app/gm on a laptop or phone. Pick a ready made game like Quick Round, Party Night or Brain Battle, or build your own. Switch on Final Wager, Team Battle or Power-ups in the Rules step if you want them. Then tap Share invite and drop the link in your group chat.

Leave Auto play on and the game runs itself: it reveals when everyone has answered or time runs out, shows standings every three questions, then moves on.
